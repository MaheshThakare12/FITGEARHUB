Complete Equipment Guide

Everything you need to know

Building the right home gym equipment list can feel overwhelming. From choosing between adjustable dumbbells and fixed free weights to deciding whether a squat rack fits your space — this guide covers every category of workout equipment so you can invest confidently, regardless of your fitness level or floor plan.

Adjustable Dumbbells

The single best investment for a compact gym, adjustable dumbbells use a dial or pin mechanism to let you swap between weights in seconds. A quality set covers 5–52.5 lb in one footprint, freeing you from a dedicated dumbbell rack. They support virtually every strength training movement: bicep curls, shoulder press, chest flyes, deadlifts, and more. If you can only buy one piece of home gym equipment, make it this.

Weight Bench

A weight bench transforms a set of free weights from a standing workout into a full upper-body training session. Adjustable benches (flat, incline, decline) expand your exercise library dramatically — enabling bench press, incline dumbbell fly, step-ups, and dips. Look for a bench rated above 300 kg that folds flat for space efficiency. A weight bench is the natural companion to adjustable dumbbells in any spare-room gym setup.

Resistance Bands

Resistance bands deliver constant tension throughout the full range of motion — something free weights cannot replicate. They effectively mimic a cable crossover machine and allow lat pulldown exercises without a dedicated gym machine. Because bands are compact, affordable, and travel-friendly, they are ideal workout equipment for apartment gyms. Use them for warm-up activation, accessory work, or as the centrepiece of an effective home workout program.

Squat Rack

The squat rack is the anchor of a serious garage gym. It holds a barbell at adjustable heights, enabling heavy squats, overhead press, barbell rows, and bench press with a safety catch — so you can train alone without a spotter. Modern folding squat racks mount to a wall and pivot flat when not in use, making them viable even in 10×12 ft spare rooms. Paired with weight stacks and a barbell, a squat rack turns your home into a legitimate strength training facility.

Cable Crossover & Lat Pulldown

A cable crossover machine uses dual adjustable pulleys and weight stacks to provide resistance at every angle — ideal for chest, shoulders, and triceps. A lat pulldown station targets the latissimus dorsi and upper back. Standalone cable machines are available in compact, wall-mounted designs suited for home gym systems. If floor space is limited, heavy-duty resistance bands anchored overhead are a practical and effective alternative for the same muscle groups.

Cardio Equipment

Effective cardio equipment for a home gym ranges from a simple jump rope to a full folding treadmill. For apartment gyms, compact rowing machines, folding bikes, and under-desk ellipticals preserve floor space while delivering genuine cardiovascular training. The FitGearHub SlimFit 2.0 treadmill folds to 8 cm thick — the slimmest folding treadmill on the market. Regular cardio combined with strength training supports full body workout goals and improves overall fitness across all fitness levels.

Garage Gyms

A garage gym offers the most freedom to build a true home gym system. With 20×20 ft or more, you can fit a squat rack, a cable crossover machine, cardio equipment, free weights, and a weight bench — the full commercial gym experience at home. Invest in rubber flooring first to protect your floor and reduce vibration. Garage gyms eliminate gym membership fees within 12–18 months and are available 24/7 for training whenever motivation strikes.

Apartment Gyms

A compact gym in an apartment doesn't require sacrificing results. With equipment that fits a 6×6 ft closet or hallway — adjustable dumbbells, resistance bands, a yoga mat, and a suspension trainer — you can perform hundreds of strength and conditioning exercises. Wall-mounted fold-down benches and under-bed treadmills extend your training options without permanent footprint. Prioritise multi-function workout equipment to maximise training variety per square foot.

Workout Equipment Buying Guide

Before purchasing any home gym equipment, consider three factors: available space, fitness goals, and budget. For strength training goals, prioritise adjustable dumbbells and a weight bench. For full body workout conditioning and fat loss, add resistance bands and cardio equipment. For a complete home gym system on a budget, start under $400 with bands, a mat, and an adjustable dumbbell set — everything else is optional until you're training consistently.

Never buy a gym membership before exploring whether home gym equipment covers your needs. Most people training 3–5 days per week on a structured programme require nothing more than adjustable dumbbells, resistance bands, a pull-up bar, and a mat. Advanced athletes building a garage gym should budget $2,000–$5,000 for a full squat rack, cable crossover, lat pulldown station, cardio equipment, and free weights setup.
